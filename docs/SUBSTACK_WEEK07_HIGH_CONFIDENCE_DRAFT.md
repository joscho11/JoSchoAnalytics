## What HIGH means

A HIGH pick has a model edge of at least three points against the frozen Tuesday U.S. median, in the direction of the pick. The gap measures points, not the chance of winning. The median sets the label. The listed sportsbook quote is the best saved Tuesday price for the side and the number used for tracking. These prices came from the October 20 snapshot, so check the current line before betting.
