**Navigation only. No analysis lives in this file** — every definition, result, caveat and conclusion
is in the notebooks. This is here so you know which one to open.

**Question:** when the `fantasy/projections` season-total model and Sleeper's preseason projection
both rank a player on the same side of his ADP, does he finish on that side?

**Status:** descriptive post-hoc research, 2026-08-02. **Not pre-registered. Not live-validated.**

- **"Is the data trustworthy?"** → 01 (hashes, generator audit, join integrity)
- **"What exactly is the signal?"** → 02 (ranks, gaps, agreement, consensus score)
- **"Does it work?"** → 03 (primary tables **and** the undrafted-tail artifact test)
- **"Does it hold up?"** → 04 (panels, seasons, positions, veteran/rookie)
- **"Is it better than chance? Better than Sleeper alone?"** → 05 ← *the decisive stage*

- **"Can I trust the whole thing?"** → 07 (reconstruction, re-hash, structural audit, verdict)

## The one-paragraph verdict

The agreement cell picks the right side of ADP well above chance (permutation null 0.483–0.505,
p = 0.0001 in all 48 cells). But the **79.9–92.3%** full-population headline is an undrafted-tail
artifact: 86–92% of those calls sit outside the draftable top 180, median ADP **634** at `t>5`. On the
drafted board it is **66.1 / 85.0 / 93.1 / 100%** at `t>0 / >5 / >7.5 / >10` on **162 / 40 / 29 / 15**
calls — and over the full 2021–2025 panel the lift over **Sleeper alone** is **+0.068 [−0.028, +0.162]**,
**+0.073 [−0.028, +0.172]** and **+0.035 [−0.095, +0.156]**, every interval crossing zero. **This study
cannot show the model adds anything to Sleeper's projection on its own.** Video-safe and
not-safe phrasings are in stage 07's conclusion.
