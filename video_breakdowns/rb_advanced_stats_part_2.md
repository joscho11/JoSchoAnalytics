# RB Advanced Stat Leaders, Weeks 1-2: Part 2

*2026 · Weeks 1-2 · running backs, the last 3 of 8 advanced stats*

**The one-liner:** Route share, target share and yards before contact for running backs through two weeks. The first two tell you who is actually in the passing game. The third is the same list as Part 1's yards after contact, in different units. All two-game, public-data numbers.

Part 1 covers the first five stats. The badge here continues that numbering (RB 6/8 to RB 8/8). This video also ends on a 10-second caveat card covering the sample, the filter, the minimums and the proxies.

---

## 1. How the boards are built

- **Source:** nflverse 2026 play-by-play through Week 2 (all 32 games), plus public snap counts. Snapshot taken 2026-09-22.
- **Garbage time:** every play is dropped when either team's win probability is above 90% before the snap, for every stat.
- **Rates come from summed counts** across both weeks, not an average of the two weekly rates.
- **Sorting** uses the unrounded value. Ties go to the larger sample, then to the name.
- **Minimums:** 20 route-proxy snaps for route share, 5 targets for target share, 8 rush attempts for yards before contact.

## 5. What this does not prove

- **That the route numbers are real route counts.** They are snaps scaled to pass plays. A back can be on the field for a pass play without running a route.
- **That a big share is a role.** Two weeks of a share can be one game script or one injury.
- **That yards before contact shows blocking.** It is 40% of yards per carry by construction, so it says nothing the yards per carry board does not.
- **That the list carries forward.** These are cumulative through Week 2. They are not projections.
